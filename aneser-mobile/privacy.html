This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Aneser Mobile (hereafter "the App") for mobile devices. The App is used to monitor mosquito sites, allowing users to navigate to pre-registered locations, fill out population/growth forms, and submit sample data.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

a. Precise Location Data

To provide core features of the application, the App requires access to your device's precise location (GPS and network-based).

  • Why we need it: To show your current location relative to pre-registered mosquito sites on Google Maps and to tag your submitted sample forms with the exact geographical coordinates of the collection site.
  • How it's handled: Your location coordinates are transmitted to our secure backend servers only when you actively submit a sample form. We do not track your location in the background when the App is closed unless explicitly required for navigation.

b. Camera and Photos

The App requests permission to access your device's camera.

  • Why we need it: To allow you to optionally capture and attach a photograph of the mosquito sample directly to the submission form.
  • How it's handled: The App only accesses your camera when you tap the photo button within the form. Photos are sent straight to our backend server alongside your form data and are not shared with third parties.

c. Form Data (User Contributions)

We collect the information you manually input into the application forms, including mosquito population counts and insect growth states.

2. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes Google Maps API to render location markers and assist with navigation. Google may collect data in accordance with their own privacy protocols. By using this App, you agree to be bound by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Security

We retain the submitted form data, photos, and location coordinates for scientific, tracking, and environmental monitoring purposes. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
